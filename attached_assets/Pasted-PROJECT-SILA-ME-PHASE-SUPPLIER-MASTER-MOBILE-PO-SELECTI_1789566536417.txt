PROJECT: SILA ME
PHASE: SUPPLIER MASTER + MOBILE PO SELECTION + GRN COMPARISON + ERP GRN POSTING

OBJECTIVE

Complete the operational Mobile receiving flow:

1. Scan Invoice
2. Mobile Basic OCR
3. Backend Advanced OCR when required
4. Resolve Supplier against Supplier Master
5. Resolve/select Purchase Order
6. Save reviewed Invoice
7. Compare Invoice Lines against OPEN PO Lines
8. User enters PHYSICAL RECEIVED quantities
9. Validate GRN
10. Submit GRN
11. POST GRN to ERP through configured POST_GRN integration
12. Receive ERP Material Document response
13. Update SILA ME GRN tables
14. Update PO/open quantities locally
15. Show GRN success in Mobile
16. Show GRN transaction in Cloud

DO NOT IMPLEMENT INVOICE POSTING.

Invoice posting is the NEXT phase.
